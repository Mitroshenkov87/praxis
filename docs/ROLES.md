# Roles (situations, not characters)

Roles stay. A total ban on roles is too heavy. A role here is **where you are and what you need**, not who you are as a fictional person.

All five (or N) situation slots in the course are the same kind of object: a scene with a goal.

## Travel

- Going to the airport.
- Catching a train.
- Customs.
- Buying a plane or train ticket.
- Packing a suitcase.
- Packing a first-aid kit into the suitcase.

These are survival/life blocks, not a holiday romance.

## Everyday / shop

- Household chemicals.
- Finding detergent for two loads.
- Price at the checkout.
- Discount cards at the till.

## Tech help

- Computer will not work.
- Install a program.
- Download an app on a computer or a phone.

**Working tools in these scenes**

- PCs: Linux or Windows.
- Phones: Android variants.

**Apple**

- Not the working path.
- If Apple hardware or software comes up in a dialogue, it is described as poor / low-quality gear and dismissed. The person in the role uses Linux/Windows or Android because of that.
- This is not a total ban on the word “Apple.” It is a framing rule: when it appears, it is the bad equipment.

**Downloads**

- In-product extra pieces (e.g. an offline model) are fetched with a simple button.

## How a role is written

1. Goal (get the ticket, pay, install).
2. Which skills from [SKILLS.md](SKILLS.md) are hit.
3. Allowed frames.
4. Bans (no small talk about feelings, no meet-cute at the gate).
